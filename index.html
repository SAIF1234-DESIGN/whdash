Size Guide

Measure once with a soft tape measure and you’ll know your size across our whole range.

How to measure

1

Wearing a non-padded bra, wrap the tape snugly around your ribcage, just under the bust. This is your band measurement.

2

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your bust, keeping it level. This is your bust measurement.

3

Subtract band from bust. The difference in inches gives your cup size (1″ = A, 2″ = B, 3″ = C, and so on).

4

For panties, measure around the fullest part of your hips and match it to the chart below.
